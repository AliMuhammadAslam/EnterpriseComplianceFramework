# Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) — Fintech Regulations

## Overview
The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) regulates the corporate sector, capital markets, insurance, NBFCs, and private pensions. For fintech, SECP's role covers digital lending by NBFCs, equity crowdfunding, regulatory sandbox, and insurtech. Key legislation includes the Companies Act 2017, NBFC Rules 2003 (amended), and the SECP Regulatory Sandbox Guidelines 2019.

## Scope
Applies to Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), investment advisors, insurance companies, crowdfunding platforms, and fintech entities operating in capital markets, lending (outside SBP jurisdiction), and insurance under SECP licensing.

## NBFC and Digital Lending Framework

### Licensing Categories
- Investment Finance Companies (IFCs)
- Leasing Companies
- Modaraba Companies
- Non-Banking Microfinance Companies (NBMFCs)
- Lending companies licensed under NBFC Rules

### Digital Lending Requirements
- SECP license mandatory for fintech lending platforms
- Minimum equity requirement as per NBFC category
- Fair lending practices: transparent pricing, no hidden charges
- Digital KYC through NADRA biometric verification
- Maximum markup rates as prescribed by SECP notification
- Monthly reporting to SECP on loan portfolio, NPLs, and recovery metrics

### Consumer Protection
- Mandatory terms and conditions disclosure before disbursement
- Cooling-off period for borrowers (varies by product)
- Prohibition on harassment during collection
- Complaint handling mechanism with SECP escalation path

## Regulatory Sandbox Framework (2019)

### Purpose
Allows fintech companies to test innovative financial products and services in a controlled environment under SECP supervision, with relaxed regulatory requirements for a limited period.

### Eligibility
- Incorporated in Pakistan under the Companies Act 2017
- Innovative product or service not adequately addressed by existing regulation
- Demonstrable benefit to consumers or the financial system
- Adequate risk mitigation plan

### Sandbox Conditions
- Testing period: up to 12 months (extendable by 6 months)
- Limited customer base during testing phase
- Regular reporting to SECP on key metrics and incidents
- Exit plan required upon sandbox completion
- Successful graduates receive appropriate regulatory license

## Crowdfunding Regulations (2019)

### Equity Crowdfunding
- Platform must be licensed by SECP as a crowdfunding operator
- Minimum paid-up capital for platforms: PKR 10 million
- Issuer eligibility: private limited companies incorporated in Pakistan
- Maximum raise per issuer: PKR 50 million in 12 months
- Individual investor limit: PKR 500,000 per offering (retail investors)
- Qualified investors: no investment limit
- Mandatory disclosure document by issuer
- Platform due diligence on issuers before listing
- Funds held in escrow until funding target met

### Peer-to-Peer Lending (proposed framework)
- SECP has published draft P2P lending rules
- Platform requires SECP license
- Risk categorization of borrowers mandatory
- Lender diversification limits
- Default management and recovery framework

## Corporate Governance Requirements

### Board Composition
- Independent directors: minimum one-third of board
- Audit Committee with majority independent directors
- Risk Management Committee at board level
- Related-party transaction approval by Audit Committee

### Reporting and Disclosure
- Annual audited financial statements
- Quarterly compliance certificates
- Material event disclosure within 24 hours
- Annual compliance review by external auditor

## Anti-Money Laundering Obligations
- SECP-regulated entities must comply with AML/CFT regulations
- Customer Due Diligence matching FATF Recommendations
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) to Financial Monitoring Unit
- Beneficial ownership disclosure for all corporate entities
- Ongoing monitoring of business relationships

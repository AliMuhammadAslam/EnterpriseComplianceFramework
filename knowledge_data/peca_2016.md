# Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) 2016 — Pakistan

## Overview
The Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 (PECA) is Pakistan's primary cybercrime legislation. It criminalizes unauthorized access to information systems, data breaches, electronic fraud, identity theft, and cyber terrorism. For fintech companies, PECA establishes the legal framework for prosecuting cybercrimes targeting financial systems and sets obligations for data protection and system security.

## Scope
Applies to any person who commits an offence under this Act within or outside Pakistan, where the offence involves a system, data, or communication in Pakistan, or causes harm to a person or entity in Pakistan.

## Key Offences Relevant to Fintech

### Unauthorized Access (Section 3)
- Unauthorized access to any information system or data
- Penalty: imprisonment up to 3 months, or fine up to PKR 50,000, or both
- Aggravated offence (with intent to gain access to government or financial system data): imprisonment up to 6 months, or fine up to PKR 100,000

### Unauthorized Copying or Transmission of Data (Section 4)
- Copying or transmitting data from any information system without authorization
- Penalty: imprisonment up to 6 months, or fine up to PKR 100,000, or both
- Particularly relevant to fintech data breaches and insider threats

### System Interference (Section 5)
- Intentionally interfering with or damaging an information system
- Includes DDoS attacks, malware deployment, or sabotage of financial systems
- Penalty: imprisonment up to 2 years, or fine up to PKR 500,000, or both

### Electronic Fraud (Section 13)
- Using any information system to interfere with any data or system with dishonest intent
- Covers phishing, card skimming, unauthorized transaction manipulation
- Penalty: imprisonment up to 2 years, or fine up to PKR 10 million, or both

### Electronic Forgery (Section 14)
- Creating or altering electronic documents, records, or data with intent to defraud
- Covers forged digital signatures, altered financial records
- Penalty: imprisonment up to 3 years, or fine up to PKR 250,000, or both

### Identity Theft (Section 16)
- Unauthorized use of another person's identity information
- Relevant to KYC fraud, synthetic identity fraud in fintech platforms
- Penalty: imprisonment up to 3 years, or fine up to PKR 5 million, or both

### Unauthorized Interception (Section 17)
- Intercepting electronic communications without authorization
- Covers man-in-the-middle attacks on financial transactions
- Penalty: imprisonment up to 2 years, or fine, or both

## Data Protection Provisions

### Right to Privacy (Section 26)
- Recognizes privacy of information stored in or communicated through information systems
- Unauthorized disclosure of personal data is an offence
- Penalty: imprisonment up to 3 years, or fine up to PKR 1 million, or both

### Data Breach Reporting
- PECA does not mandate specific data breach notification timelines
- However, SBP regulations require financial institutions to report security incidents
- FIA (Federal Investigation Agency) Cybercrime Wing handles complaints and investigations

## Regulatory Bodies Under PECA

### FIA Cybercrime Wing
- Primary investigation and enforcement body for cybercrimes
- National Response Centre for Cyber Crime (NR3C)
- Receives complaints from individuals and organizations
- Can request data preservation orders from service providers

### PTA (Pakistan Telecommunication Authority)
- Authority to block unlawful online content
- Can direct ISPs and platforms to remove content
- Issues rules for data retention by telecom operators

## Obligations for Fintech Companies

### Data Preservation
- Service providers may be required to preserve specified data for up to 90 days upon government request (Section 29)
- Financial institutions should maintain transaction logs and system access records

### Cooperation with Authorities
- Duty to cooperate with authorized officers during investigations
- Production orders for data and records (Section 30)

### Security Measures
- While PECA does not prescribe specific technical controls, the obligation to prevent unauthorized access implies implementation of reasonable security measures
- SBP's Information Security Guidelines supplement PECA requirements for financial entities

## Penalties Summary

| Offence | Section | Max Imprisonment | Max Fine |
|---------|---------|-----------------|----------|
| Unauthorized Access | 3 | 3-6 months | PKR 50,000-100,000 |
| Data Copying | 4 | 6 months | PKR 100,000 |
| System Interference | 5 | 2 years | PKR 500,000 |
| Electronic Fraud | 13 | 2 years | PKR 10 million |
| Electronic Forgery | 14 | 3 years | PKR 250,000 |
| Identity Theft | 16 | 3 years | PKR 5 million |
| Privacy Violation | 26 | 3 years | PKR 1 million |

## Relevance to Fintech Compliance
- Fintech platforms must implement security controls to prevent offences under PECA
- Customer data handling must respect privacy provisions
- Incident response plans should include FIA reporting procedures
- Employee awareness training on electronic crimes and data protection
- Third-party agreements should include PECA compliance obligations

# ISO 22301 — Business Continuity Management Systems (BCMS)

## Overview
ISO 22301:2019 is the international standard for Business Continuity Management Systems (BCMS). It specifies requirements to plan, establish, implement, operate, monitor, review, maintain, and continually improve a management system to protect against, reduce the likelihood of, prepare for, respond to, and recover from disruptions. For fintech companies, business continuity is critical given dependence on digital infrastructure, APIs, cloud services, and real-time payment processing.

## Scope
Applicable to all organizations regardless of type, size, or nature. Particularly relevant for financial services, fintech, healthcare, telecommunications, and critical infrastructure providers. ISO 22301 is certifiable and auditable.

## Key Requirements

### Clause 4: Context of the Organization
- Understand internal and external issues affecting business continuity objectives
- Identify interested parties and their requirements (regulators, customers, partners)
- Define the scope of the BCMS
- Establish the BCMS aligned with organizational strategy

### Clause 5: Leadership
- Top management commitment and accountability for BCMS
- Business continuity policy — documented, communicated, reviewed regularly
- Organizational roles, responsibilities, and authorities defined
- Management review at planned intervals

### Clause 6: Planning
- **Risk assessment**: identify threats and vulnerabilities to critical activities
- **Business Impact Analysis (BIA)**: identify critical business functions, determine Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD), and Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
- **Risk treatment**: select and implement controls to reduce disruption likelihood and impact
- Business continuity objectives: measurable, monitored, and consistent with the BC policy

### Clause 7: Support
- Resources: adequate for BCMS implementation and maintenance
- Competence: personnel trained and qualified for BC roles
- Awareness: all personnel understand the BC policy and their role during disruptions
- Communication: internal and external communication plans for incident response
- Documented information: procedures, plans, and records maintained and controlled

### Clause 8: Operation

#### Business Impact Analysis and Risk Assessment (8.2)
- Identify activities supporting products and services
- Assess impacts of disruption over time
- Set prioritized timeframes for resumption (MTPD, RTO, RPO)
- Identify dependencies: resources, suppliers, third parties, technology

#### Business Continuity Strategy and Solutions (8.3)
- Strategies to protect prioritized activities within MTPD
- Resource requirements for each strategy
- Solutions for: people, information and data, physical infrastructure, ICT, supply chain, finance

#### Business Continuity Plans and Procedures (8.4)
- Incident response structure and procedures
- Activation criteria and authority
- Communication with stakeholders during disruption
- Recovery procedures for critical activities
- Roles and responsibilities during response and recovery
- Plans must be documented, accessible, and understood by those who use them

#### Exercise and Testing (8.5)
- Regular exercises to validate BC plans (at least annually)
- Types: tabletop exercises, simulations, full-scale drills
- Post-exercise reviews and lessons learned
- Exercise results documented and used for plan improvement

### Clause 9: Performance Evaluation
- Monitoring, measurement, analysis, and evaluation of BCMS
- Internal audit program for BCMS at planned intervals
- Management review of BCMS performance, audit results, and improvement opportunities

### Clause 10: Improvement
- Address nonconformities and take corrective action
- Continually improve BCMS suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness

## Key Concepts

### Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
- Maximum time within which a critical activity must be resumed after disruption

### Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
- Maximum acceptable data loss measured in time (how current must recovered data be)

### Maximum Tolerable Period of Disruption (MTPD)
- Longest time that a disruption can last before the organization's viability is threatened

### Minimum Business Continuity Objective (MBCO)
- Minimum level of service acceptable during a disruption

## Relevance to Fintech
- Payment processing systems require near-zero RTO
- Cloud service failover and multi-region redundancy strategies
- API gateway resilience and circuit breaker patterns
- Data backup and replication for transaction data (RPO near zero)
- Vendor and third-party BC requirements in SLAs
- SBP requires financial institutions to maintain tested BC/DR plans
- Regulatory reporting obligations during and after disruptions
- Customer communication protocols during service outages

# SOC 2 — Service Organization Control 2

## Overview
SOC 2 (Service Organization Control Type 2) is a compliance framework developed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA). It defines criteria for managing customer data based on five Trust Service Criteria (TSC). SOC 2 is primarily relevant to technology and cloud computing companies that store customer data.

## Scope
Applies to service organizations that store, process, or transmit customer data, especially SaaS providers, data centers, managed IT services, and cloud-based businesses. SOC 2 Type I assesses design of controls at a point in time; Type II evaluates operational effectiveness over a period (typically 6-12 months).

## Trust Service Criteria

### Security (Common Criteria — Required)
- Protection against unauthorized access (both physical and logical)
- Firewalls, intrusion detection, multi-factor authentication
- Encryption of data at rest and in transit
- Vulnerability management and penetration testing

### Availability
- System uptime and performance monitoring
- Disaster recovery and business continuity planning
- Incident response procedures
- SLA management and capacity planning

### Processing Integrity
- System processing is complete, valid, accurate, timely, and authorized
- Quality assurance and error handling procedures
- Data validation and processing monitoring

### Confidentiality
- Data classified as confidential is protected as committed or agreed
- Encryption, access controls, and data masking
- Secure data disposal procedures
- Non-disclosure agreements

### Privacy
- Personal information is collected, used, retained, disclosed, and disposed of in accordance with privacy notice
- Consent management
- Data subject access rights
- Privacy impact assessments

## Key Controls
- Access control policies and procedures
- Change management processes
- Risk assessment and mitigation
- Incident response planning
- Vendor management
- Employee security awareness training
- Logging and monitoring
- Data backup and recovery
- Physical security controls

## Audit Process
- Conducted by an independent CPA firm
- Type I: Point-in-time assessment of control design
- Type II: Assessment of control effectiveness over 3-12 month period
- Results in a SOC 2 report shared with customers and prospects
- Reports are confidential and shared under NDA

## Consequences of Non-Compliance
- Loss of customer trust and business opportunities
- Enterprise clients typically require SOC 2 reports from vendors
- No direct legal penalties, but contractual obligations may apply
- Increased risk exposure for the organization and its customers

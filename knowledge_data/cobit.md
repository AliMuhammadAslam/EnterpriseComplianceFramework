# COBIT — Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies

## Overview
COBIT is a framework for the governance and management of enterprise information and technology (I&T), developed by ISACA (Information Systems Audit and Control Association). The current version is COBIT 2019. It provides a comprehensive framework that assists organizations in achieving their objectives for governance and management of enterprise IT, maintaining quality, control, reliability, and security of information.

## Scope
Applicable to organizations of all sizes and industries. COBIT is particularly relevant for IT governance, risk management, regulatory compliance, and enterprise IT management. It integrates well with other standards and frameworks (ISO 27001, NIST, ITIL, TOGAF).

## Core Principles (COBIT 2019)

### Principle 1: Provide Stakeholder Value
- Governance system should satisfy stakeholder needs and generate value through IT

### Principle 2: Holistic Approach
- Governance system is built from multiple components that may be of different types and work together in a holistic way

### Principle 3: Dynamic Governance System
- Governance system should be flexible and responsive to changes in enterprise design factors

### Principle 4: Governance Distinct from Management
- Governance ensures stakeholder needs are evaluated, priorities are set, and monitoring occurs
- Management plans, builds, runs, and monitors activities in alignment with governance direction

### Principle 5: Tailored to Enterprise Needs
- Governance system should be customized using design factors including enterprise strategy, goals, risk profile, and I&T-related issues

### Principle 6: End-to-End Governance System
- Covers enterprise-wide IT governance, not just the IT function

## Governance and Management Objectives

### Governance Objectives (EDM — Evaluate, Direct, Monitor)
- **EDM01**: Ensured Governance Framework Setting and Maintenance
- **EDM02**: Ensured Benefits Delivery
- **EDM03**: Ensured Risk Optimization
- **EDM04**: Ensured Resource Optimization
- **EDM05**: Ensured Stakeholder Engagement

### Management Objectives

#### APO (Align, Plan, Organize)
- Managed I&T Management Framework
- Managed Strategy, Enterprise Architecture, Innovation
- Managed Portfolio, Budget, Costs
- Managed Human Resources, Relationships, Service Agreements
- Managed Suppliers, Quality, Risk, Security, Data

#### BAI (Build, Acquire, Implement)
- Managed Programs, Requirements Definition
- Managed Solutions Identification and Build
- Managed Availability and Capacity
- Managed Organizational Change, IT Changes
- Managed IT Change Acceptance and Transitioning
- Managed Knowledge, Assets, Configuration

#### DSS (Deliver, Service, Support)
- Managed Operations, Service Requests, Incidents
- Managed Problems, Continuity, Security Services
- Managed Business Process Controls

#### MEA (Monitor, Evaluate, Assess)
- Managed Performance and Conformance Monitoring
- Managed System of Internal Control
- Managed Compliance with External Requirements
- Managed Assurance

## Design Factors
COBIT 2019 introduces design factors that influence governance system design:
- Enterprise Strategy (growth, innovation, cost optimization)
- Enterprise Goals
- Risk Profile
- I&T-Related Issues
- Threat Landscape
- Compliance Requirements
- Role of IT
- Sourcing Model for IT
- IT Implementation Methods
- Technology Adoption Strategy
- Enterprise Size

## Capability Maturity Levels
- **Level 0**: Incomplete — Process not implemented or does not achieve its purpose
- **Level 1**: Performed — Process achieves its purpose but is not well-managed
- **Level 2**: Managed — Process is planned, monitored, and adjusted
- **Level 3**: Established — Uses a defined process that achieves outcomes
- **Level 4**: Predictable — Process operates within defined limits
- **Level 5**: Optimizing — Process is continually improved

## Benefits
- Improved regulatory compliance and governance
- Better alignment between IT and business objectives
- Enhanced risk management
- Optimized IT resources and costs
- Improved decision-making through structured metrics and KPIs
